Simulated learning project · not client work

Virtual assistant operations kit

Fictional scenario: a small retail business needs inbox triage and a practical weekly workflow.

Priority queue

Urgent: Customer reports a duplicate payment — acknowledge, flag for immediate review and avoid promising a refund before verification.

Today: Supplier requests confirmation of delivery slot — check availability and reply.

Scheduled: Weekly stock report — prepare by Friday.

Sample customer reply

Subject: We received your payment enquiry

Hello, thank you for letting us know. I’m sorry for the concern this has caused. I’ve flagged your message for review. Could you share your order reference and the transaction dates (without sending full card details)? We’ll check the records and update you as soon as possible.

Kind regards,
Customer Support

Weekly workflow

Monday: review inbox and calendar. Tuesday–Thursday: follow up on open requests and update the tracker. Friday: prepare outstanding-items summary and next-week priorities.
